How It Works

An automated HR ecosystem powered by n8n workflows.

01. Recruitment Intake

Candidates submit applications and upload CVs. The workflow validates applications, stores documents in Google Drive, and records applicant information in Google Sheets.

02. AI Recruitment Screening

The system automatically analyzes CVs, evaluates candidate qualifications, and generates screening decisions.

03. Error Handling & Monitoring

Automated error logging, notifications, and monitoring ensure workflow reliability.

04. HR Policy RAG Assistant

Retrieval-Augmented Generation enables employees to ask questions and receive answers grounded in company policy documents.

05. Job Application Coach

An AI-powered coaching workflow provides personalized guidance to improve job applications.
